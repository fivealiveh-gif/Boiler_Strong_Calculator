What counts as an error
  • Raised foot touches the floor
  • Step, hop, stumble, or fall
  • Stance heel or forefoot lifts
  • Hip moves more than 30° out or forward
  • Hands leave the hips (no phone) or the phone (texting)
  • Touching the spotter, table, or wall

One error per event, even if several happen at once. Maximum 10 per trial. Stance leg is the non-dominant leg (the one they would not kick a ball with).

Educational demonstration, not a diagnostic test. The balance activity is a modified Balance Error Scoring System (single-leg stance, eyes open, with and without a phone), so published BESS norms do not apply. Handgrip percentiles are interpolated from ACSM’s Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription, 12th ed., Table 3.10 (best single hand, kg). Ages under 20 use the 20–24 row; ages over 79 use the 75–79 row. Nothing entered here is saved.
